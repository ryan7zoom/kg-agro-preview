Trade

Most of our volume comes into Bangladesh. We buy from producers in Europe, India and Australia, land it at Chattogram, and sell to processors and feed mills around Dhaka. Exports are smaller: shrimp, dried fish, processed food.

Dairy and feed come from Europe and India. Seafood arrives from Myanmar and Thailand. Rice and wheat come in by the vessel load. The things Bangladesh grows more of than it can eat, we grade, pack and ship out.

One desk owns the shipment from purchase order to delivery: letters of credit, pre-shipment inspection, phytosanitary and halal papers, customs at Chattogram, cold storage in Dhaka.

The company

Corporate office in Gulshan, registered office in Kawran Bazar.

KG Dairy & Agro Limited is a private company limited by shares, registered in Bangladesh under the Companies Act, 1994. We began in 2019 importing dairy ingredients for processors. Local sourcing, processing and export came later.

The corporate office is on Road-36 in Gulshan-2, and the registered office is at Hasan Plaza in Kawran Bazar. We hold both IRC and ERC certificates, and use the same forwarders and inspection agencies we have worked with for years.
